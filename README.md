# All-in-One Video & Music Streaming Platform

## Overview
An integrated entertainment platform designed to bring video and music streaming together in one application. The goal is to simplify content discovery and access while reducing the inconvenience of managing multiple streaming subscriptions.

## Key Features
- **Unified Streaming:** Access video and music content through a single platform.
- **Centralized Experience:** Discover and manage entertainment in one place.
- **Music Management:** Organize music and playlists.
- **Video Streaming:** Explore and watch available video content.
- **Subscription Management:** Designed to simplify the user experience around entertainment subscriptions.

## Objective
To develop a convenient, user-friendly streaming platform that combines video and music entertainment in one place and explores more affordable access to digital content.
